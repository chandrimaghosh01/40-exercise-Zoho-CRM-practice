| Module | Exercises | What You'll Master |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Module 1: Foundation** | 1–3 | Zoho CRM login, interface navigation, profile setup |
| **Module 2: Data Import** | 4–10 | Importing Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Deals, Products, Tasks, Calls |
| **Module 3: Customization** | 11–13 | Custom fields (cat info, payment status), custom "Cat Profiles" module, layout rearrangement |

Every exercise includes:
- **What you will learn** and **Why this matters**
- **Step-by-step text instructions** (no images needed)
- **Practice tasks** to reinforce the skill
- **Checkpoints** to verify completion before moving on

Plus a **Final Checklist** (15 items), **Quick Reference shortcuts table**, **Troubleshooting guide**, and a **Glossary of 21 CRM terms**.

## How to Start

1. **Create a folder** on your Desktop called `TrueHunter_Zoho_Practice`
2. **Download all 8 files** into that folder
3. **Sign up** for Zoho CRM free trial at `zoho.com/crm`
4. **Follow Exercise 1** in the curriculum (creating your account)
5. **Work through exercises in order** — each one builds on the previous

The curriculum is written for someone very new to computers, with every click explained. By Exercise 35 (the "Full KAM Week" simulation), you'll have practiced every responsibility from the TrueHunt JD in a realistic scenario. Good luck!
