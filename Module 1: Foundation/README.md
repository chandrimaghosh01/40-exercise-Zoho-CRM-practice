### EXERCISE 1: Creating Your Zoho CRM Account & First Login

**What you will learn:** How to sign up for Zoho CRM trial and understand the login process.

**Why this matters:** This is your workspace for the next 30+ exercises. You need to know how to enter and exit safely.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
2. In the address bar at the top, type: `www.zoho.com/crm`
3. Press the Enter key on your keyboard.
4. You will see the Zoho CRM homepage. Look for a button that says **"Sign Up Now"** or **"Free Trial"**.
5. Click on that button.
6. You will see a form asking for your email address. Type your personal email address.
7. Create a password. Write it down on paper. Use something you will remember like `TrueHunter2026!`
8. Click the button that says **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"**.
9. Zoho will send a verification email to your inbox.
10. Open your email account in a new tab or window.
11. Look for an email from Zoho. Open it.
12. Click the verification link inside that email.
13. You will be redirected back to Zoho. It will ask some setup questions.
14. When it asks for Company Name, type: `TrueHunter`
15. When it asks for Industry, select: `Retail` or `Consumer Goods`
16. When it asks for Company Size, select: `1-10 employees`
17. When it asks for Currency, select: `INR - Indian Rupee`
18. Click **"Finish"** or **"Continue"**.
19. You will now see the Zoho CRM dashboard. This is your home screen.
20. **IMPORTANT:** Bookmark this page. Press `Ctrl+D` on your keyboard (or `Cmd+D` on Mac). Name the bookmark `Zoho CRM`.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Log out by clicking your profile picture (top right corner) and selecting **"Sign Out"**.
- Log back in using your email and password.
- Do this 3 times until you can do it without thinking.

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you open Zoho CRM and see the dashboard within 60 seconds? If yes, you are ready for Exercise 2.

### EXERCISE 2: Understanding the Zoho CRM Interface

**What you will learn:** The names and purposes of every part of the screen.

**Why this matters:** You cannot use a tool if you do not know what the buttons do.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Log in to Zoho CRM.
2. Look at the left side of your screen. You will see a vertical bar with icons. This is called the **Navigation Panel**.
3. Move your mouse over each icon slowly. Do not click yet. A label will appear next to each icon:
   - **Home** — Your personal dashboard
   - **Leads** — People who showed interest but are not customers yet
   - **Contacts** — People you know and can contact
   - **Accounts** — Companies or subscriber families
   - **Deals** — Sales opportunities (subscriptions in our case)
   - **Activities** — Tasks, calls, and meetings
   - **Reports** — Charts and numbers
   - **Analytics** — Deeper insights
   - **Setup** — Where you change settings
4. Click on the **Leads** icon in the left panel.
5. You will see a mostly empty page with a message like "No records found." This is normal. We will add data later.
6. Look at the top of the page. You will see:
   - A **Search bar** (magnifying glass icon) — Use this to find records quickly
   - A **Create Lead** button (usually a plus sign or colored button) — Use this to add one new lead
   - **Filters** and **Views** dropdowns — Use these to see different lists
7. Click on **Contacts** in the left panel. Notice it is also empty.
8. Click on **Accounts**. Also empty.
9. Click on **Deals**. Also empty.
10. Click on **Activities** in the left panel. You will see tabs for **Tasks**, **Calls**, and **Events**.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Spend 10 minutes clicking between Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, and Activities.
- Say out loud what each section is for.
- Do not worry about the empty pages. We will fill them soon.

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you name 5 main sections of Zoho CRM and what each one stores? If yes, proceed.

### EXERCISE 3: Setting Up Your User Profile & Basic Preferences

**What you will learn:** How to make Zoho CRM feel like your own workspace.

**Why this matters:** Your profile tells the system your name, timezone, and language. This affects all timestamps on tasks and calls.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. In Zoho CRM, look at the top right corner of the screen.
2. Click on your profile picture or initials. A small menu will drop down.
3. Click on **"My Account"** or **"Profile"**.
4. You will see your profile page. Click the **"Edit"** button (usually a pencil icon).
5. Fill in these fields:
   - **First Name:** Your first name
   - **Last Name:** Your last name
   - **Phone:** Your mobile number
   - **Time Zone:** Select `Asia/Kolkata` (for Bengaluru)
   - **Date Format:** Select `DD/MM/YYYY` (Indian format)
   - **Time Format:** Select `12-hour` or `24-hour` (your preference)
   - **Language:** Select `English`
6. Click **"Save"**.
7. Go back to the profile menu (top right). Click **"Preferences"** or **"Settings"**.
8. Look for **"Email Preferences"**. Make sure your email is verified.
9. Look for **"Notification Preferences"**. Turn ON notifications for:
   - Task reminders
   - Deal stage changes
   - New lead assignments
10. Click **"Save"**.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Change your profile picture. Click on the default image. Upload any photo from your computer.
- Verify that your timezone shows correctly by creating a test task and checking the timestamp.

**CHECKPOINT:** Does your profile show the correct name, timezone, and date format? If yes, proceed.
