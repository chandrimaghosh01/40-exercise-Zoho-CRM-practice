### EXERCISE 14: Converting a Lead to a Subscriber (Lead to Account + Deal)

**What you will learn:** The most important workflow — turning an interested cat parent into a paying subscriber.

**Why this matters:** This is your core job. Every new subscription starts as a lead.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Go to **Leads**.
2. Look for a lead with status "Not Contacted." Let's use the lead `Nikhil Joshi`.
3. Click on `Nikhil Joshi` to open his lead record.
4. Read the Description: "Instagram DM. Has 3 cats, needs large packs."
5. Imagine you called Nikhil. He agreed to subscribe. Now you must convert him.
6. Look for a button at the top of the page that says **"Convert"**. It is usually a prominent button, sometimes colored.
7. Click **"Convert"**.
8. A "Convert Lead" dialog box appears. You will see three sections:
   - **Account:** Pre-filled with "Nikhil Joshi"
   - **Contact:** Pre-filled with "Nikhil Joshi"
   - **Deal:** This is where you create the subscription deal
9. In the Deal section, fill in:
    - Deal Name: `Nikhil Joshi - Monthly Subscription`
    - Amount: `2499`
    - Closing Date: Today's date (or a future date)
    - Stage: `Closed Won`
    - Type: `New Business`
10. Make sure the checkbox for **"Create Account"** is checked.
11. Make sure the checkbox for **"Create Contact"** is checked.
12. Make sure the checkbox for **"Create Deal"** is checked.
13. Click **"Convert"**.
14. Zoho will show a success message: "Lead converted successfully."
15. Click on the link to view the new Account.
16. You will see Nikhil Joshi now exists as an Account AND a Contact AND a Deal.
17. Go back to Leads. Notice that Nikhil Joshi is no longer in the Leads list. He has "graduated" to being a subscriber.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Convert 2 more leads: `Pooja Malhotra` and `Siddharth Venkat`.
- For Pooja, create a Quarterly subscription (Amount: 6999).
- For Siddharth, create a Monthly subscription with 2 cats (Amount: 4499).

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you convert a lead and verify they appear in Accounts, Contacts, and Deals? If yes, proceed.

### EXERCISE 15: Managing the Reorder Cycle (Creating Tasks for Reorders)

**What you will learn:** How to create follow-up tasks so no subscriber runs out of food.

**Why this matters:** TrueHuntererer's promise is "done-for-you ordering." You must reach out BEFORE they run out.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Go to **Accounts**.
2. Click on `Aarav Sharma`.
3. Look at the custom field "Next Reorder Date." It should be around August 15, 2026.
4. You need to call Aarav 3 days BEFORE the reorder date.
5. Look for a button that says **"Create Task"** or **"Add Task"** or a plus icon near the Activities section.
6. Click it. A "Create Task" form appears.
7. Fill in:
   - Subject: `Call Aarav - Confirm August reorder`
   - Due Date: `12/08/2026` (3 days before reorder)
   - Priority: `High`
   - Status: `Not Started`
   - Description: `Confirm SKU (Chicken Adult 2kg), quantity (1), and send payment link.`
8. Click **"Save"**.
9. The task now appears in the Activities section of Aarav's account.
10. It also appears in your main Tasks list.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Create reorder tasks for 10 subscribers.
- Use this formula: Due Date = Next Reorder Date minus 3 days.
- For Bi-weekly subscribers, the next reorder comes every 15 days.
- For Monthly subscribers, every 30 days.
- For Quarterly subscribers, every 90 days.

**ADVANCED PRACTICE:**
- Create a task for Sneha Nair (at-risk). Subject: `URGENT: Win-back call for Sneha`. Priority: High. Due Date: Tomorrow.
- Create a task for Rohan Bhat (considering cancellation). Subject: `Retention call - offer pause option`. Priority: High. Due Date: Tomorrow.

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you create a task from an Account record? Does it appear in both the Account and your main Tasks list? If yes, proceed.

### EXERCISE 16: Logging Calls and Updating Records

**What you will learn:** How to log every phone call so nothing is forgotten.

**Why this matters:** The JD says "if it isn't written down, it didn't happen." Every call must be logged.

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Go to **Accounts**.
2. Click on `Aarav Sharma`.
3. Look for the **"Calls"** section or a button that says **"Log Call"**.
4. Click **"Log Call"**.
5. Fill in:
   - Call To: `Aarav Sharma` (should auto-fill)
   - Related To: `Aarav Sharma` (Account)
   - Call Type: `Outgoing`
   - Outgoing Call Status: `Completed`
   - Call Start Time: `10/08/2026 10:30`
   - Call Duration: `5 min`
   - Subject: `August reorder confirmation`
   - Call Purpose: `Reorder`
   - Description: `Aarav confirmed 1x Chicken Adult 2kg. Payment link sent. He asked about dental treats — upsell opportunity.`
6. Click **"Save"**.
7. Now update the Account record:
   - Click **"Edit"** on Aarav Sharma's account.
   - Change Last Reorder Date to `10/08/2026`
   - Change Next Reorder Date to `10/09/2026` (30 days later)
   - Change Payment Status to `Pending` (because payment link sent but not yet paid)
   - Click **"Save"**.

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- Log 5 more calls for different subscribers.
- For each call, update the Account record with new information.
- Example: After calling Priya Patel, update her Last Reorder Date and Next Reorder Date.

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you log a call and update the account in the same session? Can you see the call history when you open the account? If yes, proceed.

### EXERCISE 18: Spotting At-Risk Accounts and Taking Action

**What you will learn:** How to identify subscribers who might leave and save them.

**Why this matters:** Retention is cheaper than acquisition. The JD says "spot at-risk accounts early and save them."

**STEP-BY-STEP:**

1. Go to **Accounts**.
2. Look at the top of the list. There is a **Filters** button or dropdown.
3. Click on **Filters**.
4. You want to find accounts that are at-risk. Since we created a Rating field, use that:
   - Select `Rating` equals `Cold`
5. Click **"Apply"**.
6. You should see accounts like Sneha Nair, Meera Desai, Rohan Bhat, etc.
7. Click on `Sneha Nair`.
8. Read her full record. Notice:
   - Rating: Cold
   - Description: "Skipped last cycle"
   - Payment Status: Overdue
   - Cat: Luna has dairy allergy
9. Create a task:
   - Subject: `Win-back call for Sneha - offer 20% discount`
   - Due Date: Tomorrow
   - Priority: High
10. Log a call (imaginary):
    - Subject: `Win-back call - Sneha`
    - Description: `Offered 20% discount on next 3 cycles. She is considering. Will decide by weekend. Follow up on Monday.`
11. Update her Account Rating to `Warm` (because she is considering).

**PRACTICE TASK:**
- For every "Cold" account, create a retention task.
- Write a different strategy for each:
  - Sneha: 20% discount
  - Meera: Payment plan option
  - Rohan: Pause instead of cancel
  - Manish: Flexible plan change
- After creating tasks, change their Rating to `Warm` to show you are working on them.

**CHECKPOINT:** Can you filter by Rating? Can you create and assign retention tasks? If yes, proceed.
